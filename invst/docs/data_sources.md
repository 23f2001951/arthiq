# Data Sources & ToS Compliance

## Active Tier (Free & Open)
- **NSE Bhavcopy:** Daily EOD prices, official and authoritative.
- **BSE Bhavcopy:** Official BSE equity bhavcopy.
- **yfinance:** Historical price series and fallback data.
- **Google / Press RSS:** Financial news headlines for sentiment analysis.

## Strictly Prohibited
- **Screener.in / Trendlyne scraping:** Explicitly forbidden by their Terms of Service. Used only for manual human cross-checks.

## Future Upgrade Path
- Clean plug-and-play adapter interfaces for paid APIs (e.g. Tijori API, Trendlyne API) when live scale demands it.
