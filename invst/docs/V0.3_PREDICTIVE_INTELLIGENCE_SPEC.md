## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

### 1.1 The Frozen Baseline: Arth IQ V0.2.1
Arth IQ V0.2.1 established an audited, mathematically reconciled, and verified deterministic research baseline:
- **CAGR Formula Reconciliation**: Complete agreement between direct compounding formula $(V_T / V_0)^{1/\text{years}} - 1$ and internal engine reporting.
- **Anti-Lookahead Boundary Guard**: Strict clamping of quarterly evaluation dates to $t \le \text{today}$.
- **Attribution & Sensitivity**: Rigorous historical market regime attribution, transaction friction ladders (0 bps to 100 bps), and factor ablation isolating the standalone impact of BQS, Sector-Valuation, Risk, and Growth.
- **Optimizer Benchmark**: Validated that Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) outperforms Equal Weighting on risk-adjusted metrics (Sharpe ratio and Maximum Drawdown reduction).

### 1.2 The Core Problem: HRP Cyclical-Regime Drag
While HRP provides superior downside protection by clustering correlated asset returns and allocating inversely to cluster variance, it exhibits **cyclical drag**:
- During explosive risk-on bull runs or sectoral momentum shifts, higher-beta cyclicals lead the market.
- HRP penalizes higher-volatility assets regardless of forward upside potential, reducing participation in strong rallies.
- Conversely, Equal Weighting suffers from catastrophic drawdowns during market corrections due to lack of risk awareness.

### 1.3 The V0.3 ML Hypothesis
Nonlinear gradient-boosted decision trees (LightGBM/XGBoost) trained on cross-sectional factor interactions, momentum ($M_{12-1}$), volatility asymmetry, and macroeconomic state variables can predict the out-of-sample forward 90-day outperformance probability:
$$y_{i,t} = \mathbb{I}\left( R_{i, [t, t+90]} - R_{\text{bench}, [t, t+90]} > 0 \right)$$
When properly calibrated via Isotonic Regression / Platt Scaling, these probabilities provide an **outperformance conviction multiplier** $m_i$:
$$w^{\text{ML}}_i \propto w^{\text{HRP}}_i \cdot \left(\frac{p_i}{0.50}\right)^\gamma$$
This yields an **ML-Tilted HRP Optimizer** that breaks HRP's cyclical drag while retaining its structural diversification and volatility reduction.

## 3. Track 1 Specification: Full Nifty 500 Scaling

### 3.1 Universe Breadth
- Expand from the 20-stock seed prototype to the full **Nifty 500** equity universe.
- Coverage across all 11 NSE sectors with sector archetype classification:
  - `FINANCIALS` (Banks, NBFCs, Insurance)
  - `TECH_SERVICES` (IT Services, Software, SaaS)
  - `CONSUMER_MOATS` (FMCG, Retail, Auto, Durables)
  - `INDUSTRIALS_CAPEX` (Capital Goods, Infrastructure, Power, Oil & Gas)
  - `HEALTHCARE_PHARMA` (Formulations, APIs, Diagnostics)
  - `COMMODITIES_CYCLICAL` (Metals, Mining, Chemicals)

### 3.2 Point-in-Time Integrity Assertions
- **Filing Lag Rules**:
  - Quarterly financial statements (Q1, Q2, Q3) become visible only after an enforced 45-calendar-day public disclosure buffer.
  - Annual/Q4 statements become visible only after an enforced 60-calendar-day public disclosure buffer.
- **Index Constituent History**: Index additions and deletions are recorded point-in-time on the effective rebalance dates to eliminate survivorship bias.

## 4. Track 2 Specification: Predictive Machine Learning Core

### 4.1 Feature Engineering (24 Signals)
1. **Fundamental Quality & Growth**:
   - $\text{ROCE}_{5y}$, $\text{ROE}_{5y}$, Debt/Equity
   - $\Delta\text{ROCE}_{\text{YoY}}$, $\Delta\text{Operating Margin}_{\text{QoQ}}$, Operating Cash Flow / EBITDA
2. **Valuation & Valuation Disconnect**:
   - Trailing P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA
   - Sector-relative valuation percentile
   - 3-year P/E median discount
3. **Price Dynamics & Momentum**:
   - $M_{12-1}$ (12-month momentum excluding last 1 month)
   - Distance from 52-week high $(\text{Price} - \text{High}_{52w}) / \text{High}_{52w}$
   - Realized 252-day annualized volatility
   - Downside semivariance & Max 5-day drawdown
   - Nifty 500 2-year rolling beta
4. **Market & Macro State Indicators**:
   - India VIX level and 30-day change
   - USD/INR exchange rate and 90-day return
   - Average news sentiment score $[-1.0, +1.0]$

### 4.2 Walk-Forward Expanding Training Protocol
- **Historical Burn-In**: 20 quarters (5 years) minimum initial training window.
- **Step Forward**: 1 quarter (90 days).
- **Zero Lookahead**: All features computed strictly as of rebalance close $t$. Target labels span $(t, t+90\text{d}]$.
- **Algorithms**:
  - LightGBM Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (`objective="binary"`, `learning_rate=0.03`, `max_depth=4`, `colsample_bytree=0.8`).
  - Scikit-Learn Calibrated Logistic Regression (L2 regularized baseline).

### 4.3 Probability Calibration & Reliability
- Raw model margin outputs are calibrated using **Platt Scaling** (`sigmoid`) and **Isotonic Regression**.
- Verification metric: **Brier Score** ($BS \le 0.22$) and empirical reliability curves across 10 deciles.

### 4.4 ML-Tilted HRP Optimizer Formulation
1. **Tree Construction**: Generate correlation distance matrix $D_{i,j} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2}(1 - \rho_{i,j})}$ and build hierarchical cluster tree via Single Linkage.
2. **Quasi-Diagonalization**: Reorder assets according to the dendrogram leaf order.
3. **Recursive Bisection Allocation**: Calculate standard HRP weights $\mathbf{w}^{\text{HRP}}$.
4. **Probability Conviction Tilting**:
   $$w^{\text{tilt}}_i = w^{\text{HRP}}_i \cdot \left( \frac{p_i}{0.50} \right)^\gamma, \quad \gamma \in [0.5, 1.5]$$
   $$w^{\text{norm}}_i = \frac{w^{\text{tilt}}_i}{\sum_j w^{\text{tilt}}_j}$$
5. **Hard Risk Constraints**:
   - Single-stock cap: $w_i \le 25\%$
   - Sector exposure cap: $\sum_{i \in \text{Sector}_k} w_i \le 40\%$
   - Excess weight redistributed proportionally across compliant names.
